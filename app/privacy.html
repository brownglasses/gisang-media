Privacy Policy — gisang uploader

Last updated: 2026-10-10

What this app is

gisang uploader is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data accessed

When the owner signs in with Google, the app requests the YouTube scopes youtube.upload (to upload videos) and youtube.readonly (to confirm which channel is signed in). It does not access any other Google data.

Data storage and sharing

The OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. The app does not collect personal data from anyone else, does not send data to any server operated by the developer, and does not sell, share, or transfer Google user data to third parties. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Via the description of the YouTube channel @rlsilhwa.
